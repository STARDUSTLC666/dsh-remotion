# Managed video template identity

The three templates are original plugin examples, not copies of upstream templates.

## Style Prompt

Readable editorial motion with generous margins, a clear headline and one focal image. Title cards and slideshows use a warm paper canvas; product cards use a midnight ink canvas and a coral accent. Motion is a short entrance and a gentle exit driven by the frame or seekable timeline, never by wall-clock time. Portrait and landscape layouts must remain readable without clipping.

## Colors

- Paper background: #FFF8ED
- Ink text / dark canvas: #142D39
- Coral accent: #F26445
- Mint secondary accent: #79CDB4
- Light text on ink: #FFF8ED

## Typography

Use the local system-ui / Microsoft YaHei / sans-serif stack. Do not fetch remote fonts. Reduce heading size for long titles; text wraps within the safe area.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not place text against the canvas edge or crop it to force a fit.
- Do not download imagery or send uploaded media to a cloud service.
- Do not use random, infinite, or wall-clock animations.
- Do not treat the Studio preview as evidence of the final encoded MP4.
